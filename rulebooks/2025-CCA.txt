RULE 8 Base Running. 8-4 FORCE-PLAY-SLIDE RULE. The intent of the force-play-slide rule is to ensure the safety of all players. This is a safety and an interference rule. On any force play, the runner must slide on the ground before the base and in a direct line between the two bases. A runner may not use a rolling or cross-body slide. The runner's raised leg must not make contact higher than the fielder's knee. The runner may not slash or kick the fielder. If a runner's slide or collision is flagrant, the runner shall be ejected.
